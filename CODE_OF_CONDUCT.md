# Code of Conduct

ProcSeal contributors are expected to communicate professionally and make the project safe for constructive technical disagreement.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and explain assumptions.
- Protect private data and never post credentials or sensitive logs.
- Give credit and respect license obligations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of secrets, and sustained disruptive conduct are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project. Security reports should follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
